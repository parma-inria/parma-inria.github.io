RESEARCH IN MOTION

Optimal transport theory
and applications

ParMA is a research team at the Inria Saclay Centre, in partnership with CNRS and Université Paris-Saclay. We study optimal transport and its applications, with a particular focus on particle methods. Our research combines mathematical modeling, numerical analysis and scientific computing to understand the geometry of probability distributions and develop efficient algorithms.

We use these ideas to design and analyze particle schemes for fluid dynamics and other evolution equations, and to address inverse problems and applications in the social sciences. By connecting theoretical results with computational tools, we aim to build reliable methods for simulating physical systems and working with data represented by point clouds.
